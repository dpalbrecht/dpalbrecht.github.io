# Ducktionary Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 21, 2026*

Ducktionary is made by David Albrecht. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Ducktionary app.

## The short version

Ducktionary does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

Your game history, games in progress, and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Word lists and definitions are built into the app, so looking up a word doesn't send anything over the internet.

## Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Ducktionary sends your scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple, and your Game Center profile and scores are handled under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We can see leaderboard scores and Game Center nicknames, the same as any other player, but we don't receive your name, email, or any other account details. You can play without Game Center by signing out in Settings → Game Center.

## Children

Ducktionary is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
