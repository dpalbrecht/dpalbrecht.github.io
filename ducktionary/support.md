# Ducktionary Support

Ducktionary is a word ladder puzzle for iPhone: change one letter at a time until you reach DUCK.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports, or a word you think is missing (or shouldn't be there)? Email **[davidpabloalbrecht@gmail.com](mailto:davidpabloalbrecht@gmail.com)** and include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I play?**
Change exactly one letter per turn. Every word you enter must be a valid English word. Reach DUCK in as few words as possible.

**How are leaderboard scores ranked?**
Fewest words wins. Ties are broken by the faster time. Each starting letter has its own leaderboard, and there's a separate Word of the Day leaderboard.

**Why didn't my score show up on the leaderboard?**
Games where you used a hint aren't submitted. You also need to be signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center). If you were offline, your score is sent the next time you open the app while connected.

**When does the Word of the Day change?**
Every day at midnight Pacific Standard Time (08:00 UTC). That's 1 am during daylight saving time. Everyone gets the same word at the same time.

**Does undo affect my score?**
No. Undo is unlimited and free.

**Where is my game history stored?**
Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your history.
